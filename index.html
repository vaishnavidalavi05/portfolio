Catering Management System — Architecture

Completed Project Web Development

Executive Overview

The Catering Management System was engineered to manage and streamline catering business operations. It digitizes the customer ordering experience, food details, billing calculations, and provides business owners with real-time analytics.

Problem Statement & Resolution

By replacing error-prone manual calculations and disorganized paper order logs with a digital interface, the project helps catering businesses avoid calculation errors, save time, and maintain reliable order histories.

Key Engineering Contributions

  • •Full UI & Web Development: Designed user interface, food menu layout, and shopping cart pages.
  • •Customer Order Management: Handled client order placement, status tracking, and confirmation.
  • •SQLite Integration: Relational schema connecting food items, transactions, and order logs.
  • •Admin Analytics Dashboard: Real-time overview of daily/monthly earnings and order archives.
  • •Automated Billing Engine: Accurate bill generation incorporating GST percentages and discounts.
