[ 01 ] — WEB DEVELOPMENT Status: Completed

Catering Management System

The Catering Management System is a full-featured web application developed to coordinate and digitize catering business workflows. It provides customers with interactive digital menus, live shopping carts, real-time bill computations, and automated order confirmations, while giving business managers a robust admin panel to track daily/monthly revenues and maintain structured order histories.

Problem Being Solved:

Traditional catering businesses struggle with manual paperwork, calculation mistakes on multi-course bookings, and cumbersome revenue tracking. This system eliminates human calculation errors through automated billing with GST and promotional discounts, saving operational hours and ensuring order clarity.

My Key Engineering Contributions:

Full-Stack Architecture: Engineered multi-page site structure from end to end.
Intuitive UI Design: Built clean, responsive food presentation views.
Interactive Menu & Cart: Developed live food item selection & cart checkout.
Automated Order Flow: Customer order processing & instant confirmation screens.
SQLite Relational DB: Schema modeling for Menu, Order, and Customer records.
Billing with GST: Algorithmic bill calculator with tax logic & discounts.
